# Shipd — Rejection List

## Never Use

- No gradient text.
- No gradients in backgrounds or UI.
- No pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
- No overlays.
- No emojis or decorative symbols.
- No `scale-1.02` or artificial hover scaling.
- No three-column feature grids.
- No generic AI visuals: glowing brains, robots, neural networks, floating 3D objects.
- No generic Web3 aesthetics: neon, cyberpunk, purple crypto gradients, excessive glow.
- No glassmorphism.
- No excessive rounded cards or pill-shaped UI.
- No unnecessary animations or decorative motion.
- No oversized decorative illustrations that do not communicate product functionality.
- No generic startup copy such as “Launch your…”, “Unlock…”, “Supercharge…”, or “The future of…”.
- No visual elements added only to fill empty space.
- No visual decoration that competes with the product or its data.

## Principles

- Prefer clarity over decoration.
- Prefer real product evidence over abstract visuals.
- Prefer restrained motion over spectacle.
- Prefer structured layouts over card-heavy compositions.
- Every visual element must have a clear purpose.
