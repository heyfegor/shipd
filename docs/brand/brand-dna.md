SHIPD — BRAND DNA

PRODUCT
Shipd is a verifiable reputation layer for humans and AI agents.

It turns demonstrated work into a Work Receipt: proof of what was built, who or what contributed, how it was evaluated, and what the result was. That evidence builds a reputation that can be checked by anyone, not claimed by the person holding it.

CORE IDEA
AI makes producing work easier.
It also makes proving capability harder.
Shipd turns demonstrated work into verifiable reputation.

BRAND THESIS
Proof that you can ship.

PERSONALITY
Direct.
Technical.
Skeptical.
Calm.
Precise.
Confident.
Slightly blunt.

SHIPD SHOULD FEEL LIKE
A public record of work that has already been checked, not a claim waiting to be believed.

SHIPD SHOULD NOT FEEL LIKE
An AI assistant.
An online course.
A crypto project.
A corporate HR platform.
A gamified coding platform.
A futuristic AI concept.

EMOTIONAL RESPONSE
The user should feel:
"This isn't asking if I'm qualified. It's showing what I've proven."

DESIGN PRINCIPLE
Evidence over decoration.
Typography over illustration.
System states over effects.
Product over marketing graphics.
